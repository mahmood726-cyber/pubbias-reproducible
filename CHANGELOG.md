# Changelog

## 1.0.0

Initial local validation archive of allmeta commit 3dcc679. Preserves final app, R reference, corpus, disposition, original comparison evidence and actually used benchmark scripts. Adds regenerated tables, figures, expected-value assertions and a separately rerun Hackshaw REML example.

The upstream repair corrected Begg exact/tied-rank handling, raw binary counts for Peters/Harbord and trim-and-fill REML iteration, and labelled selection experimental. Six non-selection exceptions remain. This archive does not claim a new full validation run, certification, publication or deployment.
